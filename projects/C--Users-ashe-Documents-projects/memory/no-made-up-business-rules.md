Ashe (2026-11-26, production board): "dont make anything up except I approve or Gabi does. This should be in your email and we can explain to her why we made it up and what it does."

It was triggered by board column names I had invented ("To pick → Picked & packed → Ready to ship" etc.) and presented without saying they were guesses.

**Why:** the app must reflect how AVW really works. Invented steps mislead users and look like decisions nobody made.
**How to apply:**
- Don't invent process steps, column/stage names, business rules, labels or thresholds. Ask Ashe, or mark the item as a PLACEHOLDER awaiting approval.
- When a placeholder is needed to build/demo, say so at the time it's shown, and add it to the project's approval list (for the production board: NOTES-FOR-GABI.md "Placeholders we chose").
- Each placeholder gets: what we assumed, why, what it does, and what we need from them.
