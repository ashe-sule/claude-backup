Ashe asked (2026-10-17) why I missed a DocuSign/PandaDoc-style e-sign platform until they suggested it. Cause: I anchored on the categories Ashe named (IT/security, car wash, ERP, labels, ticketing) and on portfolio/showcase value, and never scanned horizontal business software by spend.

**Why:** Ashe builds products to sell ([[github-buildup]]); the best ideas are often generic tools every company already pays for.
**How to apply:** When brainstorming, run these lenses before answering: (1) "what subscriptions does every business pay for?" (e-sign, scheduling, forms, invoicing, HR/onboarding, password manager, status pages, etc.), (2) what Ashe's own company pays for, (3) what connects to the existing suite. Rank by buildability with free tools, sellability, and IP risk. Say plainly what lens produced each idea.
