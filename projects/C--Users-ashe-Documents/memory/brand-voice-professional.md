Product taglines and marketing copy must sound professional and institutional (law firm / bank tone), not catchy or casual.

**Why:** Ashe rejected Sealed's "Get it signed today. Prove it forever." as "a bit unprofessional" (2026-10-19) and picked "Signed, sealed and verifiable."

**How to apply:** When proposing names, taglines or hero copy for any NexuCentri product ([[nexucentri]]), lead with calm, precise, formal options; offer 3–4 choices via a question before committing.
