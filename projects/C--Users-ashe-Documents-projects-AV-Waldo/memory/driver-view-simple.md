The driver view must be as simple as possible, with very little for the driver to do. Drivers are not tech-savvy (said by Ashe on 2026-11-13).

**Why:** Kenny and future drivers aren't comfortable with tech. Every extra button or decision is a chance for mistakes.
**How to apply:** Put the work on the system and dispatch, not the driver. Use big buttons, one main action per screen, plain words and no jargon. Don't add driver-side choices: route changes are approved by dispatch, and the driver just gets told. Review every driver-facing change against this rule, and cut steps where possible (candidates: fewer proof fields, automatic arrival from GPS later).
