Ashe (2026-10-17): "on this portfolio project we are on to a few things. 1 is build ASHE and another is build AVW. I want both to go different ways but I want to be able to help AVW grow too. So money making ideas for ASHE AND GROWTH FOR AVW and also help them make more money on a very large and bigger scale."

**Why:** Ashe wants personal products to sell AND to be the person who scales AVW Equipment (car wash equipment maker).
**How to apply:** Every idea gets tagged ASHE or AVW. ASHE track = products any business buys, not car-wash specific (lower employer-IP risk). AVW track = car-wash/AVW-specific growth, built as AVW projects in AVW repos, owned by AVW. Never mix code or data between tracks. Flag IP/conflict-of-interest risk when an ASHE idea overlaps AVW's business. Related: [[github-buildup]], [[product-ideas-method]].

**Update 2026-10-17:** Ashe is allowed to build and sell on their own. Ashe wants to build products first and decide LATER, per product, whether AVW gets it (offer/license to AVW) or it stays ASHE-only. So: build everything as Ashe-owned (own time/PC/accounts, no AVW data/code); don't pre-split projects into tracks.
